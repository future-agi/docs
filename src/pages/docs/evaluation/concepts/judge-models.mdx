A judge model is the model that reads a response and applies an eval template's criteria to produce a result and a reason. It receives the text to evaluate, the template's rule, and the required inputs, then returns a verdict. A judge only reads and scores; it never generates or edits your AI's output. Agent Evaluator and LLM-as-Judge evals use a judge; Code Evals do not.

## Future AGI judge models

Future AGI ships proprietary models built for evaluation, not for general-purpose chat:

| Model | Code | Inputs | Best for | Latency |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| TURING_LARGE | `turing_large` | Text, image, audio | Max accuracy, multimodal evals | Higher |
| TURING_SMALL | `turing_small` | Text, image | High fidelity at lower cost | Medium |
| TURING_FLASH | `turing_flash` | Text, image | Fast, high-accuracy evals | Low |
| PROTECT | `protect` | Text, audio | Safety, guardrails, user rules | Low |
| PROTECT_FLASH | `protect_flash` | Text | First-pass binary filtering | Ultra-low |

## Bring your own judge

You can also use your own model as the judge, from OpenAI, Bedrock, SageMaker, Vertex AI, Azure, or a custom endpoint. Reach for a custom judge when you need a domain-tuned model, must keep inference in a region, or already pay for a model you want to reuse. See [Use custom models](/docs/evaluation/features/custom-models).

## Modality

A judge can only score inputs it can read. Image evals need `turing_large` or `turing_small`; audio evals need `turing_large` or `protect`. A text-only judge cannot score an image or an audio input, so pick a multimodal judge when the eval carries one.

## How to choose

| Situation | Judge |
|---|---|
| Maximum accuracy over speed | `turing_large` |
| High quality at a reasonable cost | `turing_small` |
| Large runs where speed matters | `turing_flash` |
| Safety and guardrail checks | `protect` or `protect_flash` |
| Image or audio inputs | a multimodal judge (see Modality) |
| Domain-specific or compliance need | a custom judge |
