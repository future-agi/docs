Evaluations measure how well your AI achieves the user's goal, scored on a metric like task completion, factual accuracy, safety, or tone. You define what "good" means once, and Future AGI scores every response against it, returning a result and a plain-language reason for each one.

Evaluation is the middle of a loop, not a standalone tool: you observe what your agent did, evaluate its quality, optimize the prompt or model, and enforce a bar before anything ships. The same eval runs everywhere your work lives, so a score means the same thing on a dataset, a live trace, or a pull request.

Four objects carry the whole product: a [template](/docs/evaluation/concepts/eval-templates) defines what to measure, a config points it at your data, a run executes, and a [score](/docs/evaluation/concepts/eval-results) comes out. Future AGI ships 156 built-in evals, or you write your own.

## How it connects

Evaluation sits in the middle of Future AGI and feeds on the rest: [Observe](/docs/observe) turns live traces into eval inputs and shows scores back on the trace, datasets and simulations are where offline evals run, optimization consumes scores to improve a prompt, and thresholds become production gates.
