This is the annotator's side of a [queue](/docs/annotations/concepts/queues-and-items): open an item, read its source, answer every [label](/docs/annotations/concepts/labels) the queue carries, and submit. What submitting does depends on the queue. Your answers can finish the item, or go to a reviewer first.

<Prerequisites title="Before you start">
- The queue has to be active. The one exception is resuming skipped items once the queue is completed.
- Only annotators and managers can submit an annotation. Holding the reviewer role by itself doesn't grant that; see [Queue settings & limits](/docs/annotations/reference/queue-settings-and-limits).
- Unless the queue auto-assigns items to all annotators, the item has to be assigned to you.
</Prerequisites>

## Open the assigned item

1. Open the queue from **Annotations > Queues**.
2. Click **Start Annotating** to open the next available item.

   Once the queue is complete and has skipped items left, the button reads **Resume Skipped** instead. To work on a particular item, open it from the **Items** tab; [Explore a queue](/docs/annotations/guides/explore-queue) covers both.

Either way you land in the annotation workspace on a specific item. It splits into two resizable panes: the source on the left and the labels on the right. Drag the divider if you want more room for either side.

If you get a message instead of an item, see [Troubleshooting](#troubleshooting).

## Read the source and answer the labels

The left pane renders whatever the item points to: a [trace](/docs/observe/concepts/traces), a [span](/docs/observe/concepts/spans), a [session](/docs/observe/concepts/sessions), a [dataset row](/docs/dataset/concepts/understanding-datasets), a prototype run or a voice call. Judge it before you answer anything on the right.

1. Read the source in the left pane.
2. Read the queue's instructions, if its creator wrote any.

   They sit in a collapsible section above the labels and are open by default. Collapse them once you know the guidance.

3. Answer every label under the **Labels** heading in the right pane.

   Each label's control depends on its type; [Label types & values](/docs/annotations/reference/label-types-and-values) has the exact constraints. You can't submit until every label has an answer. Leave one blank and the submit button stays disabled.

4. Optionally, add free-text context in **Notes** ("Add notes for this item..."), below the labels.

   A note is your own commentary alongside the labels, not a label itself.

## Submit and check the state

1. Click the submit button. Its text tells you what submitting will do:

   | Button | What it does |
   | --- | --- |
   | **Submit & Next** | The default. Saves your answers and moves to the next item. |
   | **Submit for Review** | Shows when the queue requires reviewer approval. Your answers go to a reviewer before the item counts as done. |
   | **Update & Next** | Shows when you're revising an answer you already submitted. |

2. Click **Back to Queue** in the header to return to the queue's detail view.
3. Find the item on the **Items** tab and check its status.

   An item is done once the queue's required number of annotators have answered it. On a queue that requires reviewer approval, it sits in pending review until a reviewer approves it.

If a reviewer sends an item back with feedback, it shows up in the header's **Comments** button and as a **Reviewer feedback** alert at the top of the right pane. Revise the answer and submit again with **Update & Next**.

## Skip or revisit an item

- **Skip:** click **Skip** in the header, or press **S**. A skipped item isn't marked complete. It steps out of your way so you can come back to it later. **Skip** is disabled once an item is completed or pending review. On a completed item, the button's tooltip explains why.
- **Previous** and **Next:** in the footer, beside a position indicator (`n / total`).
- **Show completed:** toggles whether items you've already finished are included as you move through the list.

Keyboard shortcuts:

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Tab / Shift+Tab | Move between labels. |
| 1-9 | Select a categorical option or star rating. |
| Cmd/Ctrl+Enter | Submit and move to the next item. If labels are unanswered, a reminder names them. |
| S | Skip the item. |
| ← / → | Move to the previous or next item. |
| ? | Toggle the shortcut overlay. |

## Submit from code

From code, annotating an item takes three calls: read the item's detail to get each label's allowed values, submit the answers, then complete the item. Submitting alone leaves the item in progress.

- **Python and TypeScript:** the [queue item SDK methods](/docs/sdk/reference/annotation-queues#annotation_queuesitemssubmit) cover all three.
- **REST:** [Submit Item Annotations](/docs/api/annotations/items/submit-annotations) and [Complete Annotation Queue Item](/docs/api/annotations/items/complete-item) are separate requests.
- **MCP:** the [MCP server](/docs/falcon-ai/guides/use-the-mcp-server) tools are `get_annotation_item_detail`, `submit_annotation` and `complete_annotation_item`.

## Troubleshooting

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Item Reserved** | Someone else already has that item open. Click **Skip to Next Item**. |
| **Queue Not Active** | The queue isn't active. A manager has to reactivate it. |
| **Assigned to {`{name}`}** | The item belongs to someone else, and a queue manager controls that assignment. Click **Skip to Next Item**. |
| **All Done!** | Nothing is left assigned to you. |
| The submit button is disabled | A label has no answer yet. Press Cmd/Ctrl+Enter and a reminder names exactly which labels are missing. |
| A numeric or text answer is rejected | Answers are checked against the label's configured bounds, and the check runs again on the server when you submit. Bring the value back within the label's min, max, step or length and submit again. |
