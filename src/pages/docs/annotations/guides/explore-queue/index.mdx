title: "Explore a queue"
description: "Find your way around a queue's detail view: its header, tabs, and toolbar actions"
---

Once a queue has items and annotators in it, this detail view is where the work actually happens. This guide walks that view using `Support quality review`, the queue built in the Create a queue guide, as the example; any queue of your own works the same way.

These guides all start from a queue you've already created. If you don't have one yet, [Create a queue](/docs/annotations/guides/create-queue) makes the first one.

## Open the queue

Go to **Annotations → Queues** and click anywhere on the `Support quality review` row. The whole row is the target, so there's no separate open action to find.

## Read the header

The header carries the queue's name, a status badge next to it, and progress underneath. The badge reads **Draft**, **Active**, **Paused**, or **Completed**, and it's the one place to check whether the queue is unlocked for annotating before you try to open an item. If you have items assigned to you, you get two progress bars: your own progress first, then the queue's overall progress with a breakdown of how many items are pending, in progress, in review, or skipped.

## The five tabs

Below the header, the view splits into five tabs:

- **Items**, the list of items in the queue and where you open one to annotate it
- **Settings**, the queue's configuration, managers only
- **Analytics**, performance across the queue
- **Agreement**, how consistently annotators score the same items
- **Rules**, automation rules that feed items into the queue, managers only

**Settings** and **Rules** only show up if you're a manager on the queue. Everyone else sees Items, Analytics, and Agreement.

## When you can't annotate

- **You can only annotate while the queue is active.** Try to open an item outside that state and you're told to manage the status from the Settings tab first. If you're a manager, the toolbar's **Activate** button does the same thing in one click whenever the queue isn't already active; use Settings for any other status change, or if you don't see the button.
- **You can only open an item assigned to you, unless auto-assign is on.** Auto-assign is a toggle in the queue's Settings tab, the same one you set when you built the queue in [Create a queue](/docs/annotations/guides/create-queue#add-annotators). With it off, an item assigned to someone else stays closed to you even if you can see it in the list.

## The toolbar

Four actions live in the header toolbar, and which ones you see depends on your role and the queue's state:

- **Activate** shows up for managers only, and only while the queue isn't already active
- **Export** opens a menu with **Download** and **Export to Dataset**. It's grayed out until the queue has items in it
- **Review Items** (or **View Submissions**, depending on whether the queue requires review) is for reviewers and managers, once there's something to review
- **Start Annotating** (or **Resume Skipped**, once the queue is complete and has skipped items left) opens the annotation workspace for anyone who can annotate, picking the next available item for you. Opening an item straight from the **Items** tab lands you in that same workspace, just on the item you clicked instead of the next one in line
