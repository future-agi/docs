title: "Add items"
description: "Pull sources into a queue and hand them to the right people"
---

A [queue](/docs/annotations/concepts/queues-and-items)'s **Items** tab is empty until you put something in it. This guide covers getting items into `Support quality review`, the running example from [Create a queue](/docs/annotations/guides/create-queue), then filtering and assigning them once they're there. Adding and assigning are both manager work: if you're not a manager on the queue, you won't see these controls at all.

## Add items

Open `Support quality review` and switch to the **Items** tab. **Add Items** sits in the toolbar above the item table, next to the item filters covered below. Click it to open the picker: two ways to fill the queue, hand-pick specific items or set a filter and add everything it matches. Hand-pick when you already know the exact items you want; use filter mode when you want everything matching a rule, however many that turns out to be.

### Hand-pick items

1. Choose where the items come from: datasets, traces, spans, sessions, or simulation recordings
2. Use the checkbox column to select the specific rows you want
3. Click **Add to queue**

### Add by filter

1. Choose a source the same way as hand-picking
2. Set the filters that describe what you want, using the filter controls above that source's table
3. Instead of checking rows one by one, use the table's select-all control to switch into filter mode
4. Click **Add to queue**. The queue resolves everything the filter matches on the server at that moment, not just what's loaded on screen

<Note>
Hand-picking tops out at 1,000 items per Add Items call. Go over that and the request is rejected outright with an HTTP 413, so split the selection into batches under 1,000, or switch to Add by filter instead. Filter mode has more headroom, but the full caps and gated behavior live on [Queue settings & limits](/docs/annotations/reference/queue-settings-and-limits).
</Note>

Two things worth knowing about what happens once items are added:

- **Duplicates are silently skipped.** If a source is already in the queue, or shows up twice in what you're adding, it's dropped with no error and no second item
- **Assignment can happen automatically.** If the queue has [auto-assign](/docs/annotations/reference/queue-settings-and-limits) turned on, or its [assignment strategy](/docs/annotations/reference/queue-settings-and-limits) is Round Robin or Load Balanced, incoming items get an assignee the moment they land. Otherwise they arrive unassigned, and someone has to assign them by hand

<Note>
An item's preview is a snapshot taken the moment it's added, not a live read of the source. If the underlying trace or row changes afterward, what you see on the item doesn't follow it.
</Note>

## Filter and find items

Above the item table sit three filters, item status, source, and review status, plus a **My Items** toggle that narrows the table down to whatever's assigned to you.

## Assign and remove items

Select one or more rows and two more buttons join that same toolbar: **Assign Selected** and **Remove Selected**, both scoped to your selection.

**Assign Selected** opens the Assign Selected Items dialog, and like adding items, it's manager-only. You can add an assignee to what's already there, replace it outright, or clear it, but only for people who are already members of the queue: you can't assign an item to someone who hasn't been added yet.

**Remove Selected** takes the selected items out of the queue entirely, along with any annotations already submitted on them. There's no undo: getting an item back means adding it again as a new item, starting from zero submissions.
