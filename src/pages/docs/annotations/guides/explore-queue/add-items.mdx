---
title: "Add items"
description: "Pull sources into a queue and hand them to the right people"
---

A [queue](/docs/annotations/concepts/queues-and-items)'s **Items** tab is empty until you put something in it. This guide covers getting items into `Support quality review`, then filtering and assigning them once they're there. Adding and assigning are both manager work: if you're not a manager on the queue, you won't see these controls at all.

## Add items

Open `Support quality review` and switch to the **Items** tab. **Add Items** opens a dialog with two ways to fill it: hand-pick specific items, or point it at a saved filter and let the queue resolve everything that matches on the server at the moment you add.

<Note>
An Add Items call tops out at 1,000 items when you're picking them by hand. Go over that and the request is rejected outright with an HTTP 413. Filter mode has more headroom, but it isn't unlimited either: whatever the filter resolves to, only the first 10,000 matches come in.
</Note>

Two things worth knowing before you click Add:

- **Duplicates are silently skipped.** If a source is already in the queue, or shows up twice in what you're adding, it's dropped with no error and no second item
- **Assignment can happen automatically.** If the queue has auto-assign turned on, or its assignment strategy is Round Robin or Load Balanced, incoming items get an assignee the moment they land. Otherwise they arrive unassigned, and someone has to assign them by hand

<Note>
An item's preview is a snapshot taken the moment it's added, not a live read of the source. If the underlying trace or row changes afterward, what you see on the item doesn't follow it.
</Note>

## Filter and find items

Above the item table sit three filters, item status, source, and review status, plus a **My Items** toggle that narrows the table down to whatever's assigned to you.

## Assign and remove items

Select one or more rows and the toolbar swaps in **Assign Selected** and **Remove Selected**, both scoped to your selection.

**Assign Selected** opens the Assign Selected Items dialog, and like adding items, it's manager-only. You can add an assignee to what's already there, replace it outright, or clear it, but only for people who are already members of the queue: you can't assign an item to someone who hasn't been added yet.

**Remove Selected** takes the selected items out of the queue entirely.
