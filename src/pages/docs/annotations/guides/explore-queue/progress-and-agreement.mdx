A completed [queue](/docs/annotations/concepts/queues-and-items) item doesn't tell you whether its annotators agreed. Use **Analytics** to track the work and **Agreement** to compare the [scores](/docs/annotations/concepts/scores) people submitted for the same [label](/docs/annotations/concepts/labels) on the same item.

<Prerequisites title="Before you start">
- A queue with submitted work. This guide uses `Support quality review`, the queue from [Create a queue](/docs/annotations/guides/create-queue), which carries the `Response quality` label and requires two submissions per item.
- For the **Agreement** tab, the Agreement Metrics entitlement. Agreement is a gated feature: without the entitlement, the tab comes up empty instead of loading.
- For an agreement calculation, [submissions per item](/docs/annotations/reference/queue-settings-and-limits) above the default of 1, and actual submissions from different annotators on the same item and label. Assignment alone doesn't count.
</Prerequisites>

## Check how much work is done

1. Open `Support quality review`.
2. Select the **Analytics** tab.

   **Analytics** and **Agreement** sit after **Items** on the queue's detail view. Managers also see **Settings** there.

3. Compare **Completed** with **Total Items**.

   **Completion Rate** expresses those counts as a percentage. **Avg / Day** averages completions over the last 30 days.

4. Inspect the status breakdown for unfinished work.

   The six buckets are **Completed**, **In Review**, **Needs Changes**, **Resubmitted**, **Pending Annotation**, and **Skipped**. **In Review** holds items part-way to the queue's required submissions and, on a review-enabled queue, items that have reached those submissions but are still waiting on a reviewer's verdict. On this queue, the first annotator's submission alone puts the item in **In Review**, with no reviewer needed. **Needs Changes** and **Resubmitted** come out of the review workflow: an item only moves through them when the queue requires reviewer approval.

5. Look at **Daily Throughput (Last 30 Days)** for days when completions slowed or picked up.
6. Inspect **Label Distribution** for the `Response quality` counts: `Good`, `Needs work`, and `Wrong`.

   Numeric and star labels group by rating; thumbs labels group by up or down. The distribution shows how often a value was picked, not whether it was picked by two people on the same item.

7. Check **Annotator Performance** to see who contributed.

   Once an item meets the submission requirement across all required labels, every contributing annotator gets credit for completion, not just the last one.

## Compare judgments on the same work

1. Select **Agreement**.
2. Read **Overall Agreement**.

   The card shows one percentage: the share of item/label pairs where every annotator who scored it landed on the same value.

3. Inspect **Per-Label Agreement** for `Response quality`.

   The section has one row per label. **Agreement** is the same raw percentage, scoped to that one label. **Disagreements** counts how many items its annotators didn't match on.

4. Compare rows in **Annotator Pair Agreement**.

   Each row is a pair of annotators with overlapping work, with their agreement percentage. **Comparisons** counts the item/label comparisons they share, not items, so one item with three labels counts as three. It's the fastest way to tell an annotator who disagrees with everyone else apart from a label that's just genuinely hard to agree on.

### Interpret Cohen's Kappa

Cohen's Kappa is the only agreement statistic the tab reports, whether two annotators scored an item or five. It corrects the raw Agreement percentage for how often annotators would land on the same value purely by chance, so it usually reads lower than Agreement alone, especially on a label with few options.

Kappa only computes for categorical, numeric, star, and thumbs [label types](/docs/annotations/reference/label-types-and-values). A free-text label shows a dash instead.

As a rough guide, values below 0.20 indicate poor agreement, 0.21 to 0.40 fair, 0.41 to 0.60 moderate, 0.61 to 0.80 substantial, and above 0.80 almost perfect.

## If no percentage appears

| What you see | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| An empty **Agreement** tab | Check the Agreement Metrics entitlement. |
| **N/A** with “Need at least 2 annotators per item to calculate agreement” | Check that two different people submitted the same label on the same item, not just that both were assigned. |
| A percentage below 100% | There is overlap, but at least one item/label pair has different values. Inspect the label and annotator-pair breakdowns. |

For programmatic access, use the queue's [Analytics endpoint](/docs/api/annotations/queues/get-analytics) for throughput and the [Agreement endpoint](/docs/api/annotations/queues/get-agreement) for the comparison.
