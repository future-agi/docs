Every conversation you have with Falcon AI is saved, so you can find a thread by its first message and pick it up again. Both the side panel and the full page show your history, but only the full page lets you rename or delete a chat.

## Find a saved chat

1. In the side panel, select **Chat history** in the header.

   You should see a popover with **Search chats...** above the list; on the full page the same list is already visible in the left rail.

2. Type part of the title into **Search chats...**.

   The list filters as you type; unless you renamed it, a chat's title is the first message, shortened to fit the rail.

3. Select the matching conversation to resume it.

A new thread appears in history as soon as you send its first message. If the connection drops while Falcon AI is answering, it replays whatever you missed on its own once the connection comes back.

## Rename a chat you want to keep

Titles come from your first message, so the rail reads as a list of questions rather than summaries. Rename any thread you expect to come back to.

1. Open the Falcon AI full page if you're in the side panel.
2. Hover over the conversation in the left rail.
3. Select the **⋯** menu at the right edge of its row.

<img src="/images/docs/falcon-ai/guides/manage-conversations/row-menu.png" alt="A conversation row in the left rail with its row menu open, showing Rename above Delete in red" style={{ borderRadius: '5px' }} />

4. Choose **Rename**.
5. Enter the new title in **Rename conversation**.

   Look for the new title in the left rail the next time you search for this thread.

## Delete a saved chat

1. On the full page, find the conversation in the left rail.
2. Hover over its row.
3. Open the **⋯** menu.

<Warning>
Check the title before choosing **Delete**. There is no confirmation prompt.
</Warning>

4. Choose **Delete**.

   The row disappears immediately; if that conversation was open, you should see a new, empty chat.

## Start over without deleting

Select **New chat** in the full-page rail or the side panel's header. Use this when you want a fresh thread without removing the old one.
