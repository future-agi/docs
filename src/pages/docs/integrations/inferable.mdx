---
title: "Inferable tracing support"
description: "Learn about the current status of Inferable tracing with Future AGI."
---

Future AGI does not currently support tracing Inferable runs and tool calls. Inferable's Langfuse integration does not export OTLP traces to Future AGI, so do not enter Future AGI credentials or the Future AGI collector URL in its Langfuse settings.

Inferable's [**Send Message Payloads** setting](https://langfuse.com/integrations/other/inferable#message-payload-security) adds prompts, responses, tool arguments and tool results to the data sent to the configured Langfuse host. Leave it off unless you intend to share that content with that host; turning it off still sends run metadata.

For model-provider calls made directly in your own application process, see the [traceAI integrations](/docs/integrations/traceai). Instrumenting that process does not capture calls made in Inferable's service or reconstruct Inferable's run and tool-call trace.
