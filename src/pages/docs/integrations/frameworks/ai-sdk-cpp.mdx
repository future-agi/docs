## Tracing availability

[AI SDK C++ at source commit `9d8cd1`](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ai-sdk-cpp/tree/9d8cd18200a33b64460c9101ed0dd6d4988bea61) is not supported as a direct tracing integration with Future AGI. Its `ai::langfuse` client sends Langfuse ingestion batches, not the OTLP traces Future AGI receives. Do not point its Langfuse host at a Future AGI trace endpoint.

The Langfuse client captures prompts, tool arguments and results, model output, and provider error text; it does not provide a switch to omit that content. Review your data-handling requirements before enabling it for a Langfuse destination.
