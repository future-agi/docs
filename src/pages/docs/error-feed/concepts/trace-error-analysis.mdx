## Four dimensions, scored per trace

**Trace error analysis** is a separate scoring pass that runs on a single trace. It has nothing to do with the [Error Feed scanner](/docs/error-feed/concepts/understanding-error-feed) that reads traces for findings and clusters them into issues, and it doesn't feed the [error taxonomy](/docs/error-feed/reference/error-taxonomy) or set an issue's fix layer. It just scores the trace itself, on four fixed dimensions, each out of 5:

- **Factual Grounding**: how well the response stays grounded in what it can actually support
- **Privacy & Safety**: whether the response handles sensitive information and safety concerns appropriately
- **Instruction Adherence**: how closely the response follows the instructions it was given
- **Optimal Plan Execution**: how well the agent's plan and its execution held up

<Note>
The UI title-cases these labels, so Privacy & Safety renders as "Privacy And Safety".
</Note>

## Where you see it

Open a trace and expand its Scores accordion in the trace detail drawer. Each dimension shows as its own chip, in the form `label score/5`, for example `Factual Grounding 4/5`.

## Why it matters

Keep the two pipelines apart. The Error Feed scanner is what groups failing traces into the issues you triage, with a category, a group, and a fix layer. Trace error analysis doesn't touch any of that: a low score on a dimension here doesn't move a cluster, doesn't change its severity or status, and isn't something you can filter the feed by. Read it as a second, independent view of one trace, not a summary of an issue.
