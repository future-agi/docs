## Four scores, one trace at a time

Every trace you open in Observe can carry its own **Error Analysis**: four quality scores, each out of 5, computed for that one trace. The four dimensions are:

- **Factual Grounding**: how well the response holds up against the evidence the agent actually had
- **Privacy & Safety**: how well the response holds up on privacy and safety practice
- **Instruction Adherence**: how closely the response follows the instructions it was given
- **Optimal Plan Execution**: how well the agent's plan and tool use got the job done

## Where you see it

Open a trace and expand the Scores accordion in the trace detail drawer. Each dimension renders as a chip reading "label score/5", for example "Factual Grounding 4/5".

<Note>
The UI title-cases the raw dimension names, so Privacy & Safety renders literally as "Privacy And Safety" in that accordion.
</Note>

## Not part of the feed

This is a separate pipeline from the scanner behind [Error Feed](/docs/error-feed/concepts/understanding-error-feed). It doesn't feed the [taxonomy](/docs/error-feed/reference/error-taxonomy) that decides an issue's category and fix layer, and it doesn't drive any filter in the feed. A trace can sit inside an open issue and still score well here, or score badly here without ever surfacing as an issue: the two are independent reads on the same trace.

## Why it matters

The feed answers what's recurring and where to fix it. Error Analysis answers how one specific trace performed on qualities a scanner finding doesn't capture on its own. Reading them together only works by checking the same trace yourself: nothing in the product links a low score to an open issue for you.
