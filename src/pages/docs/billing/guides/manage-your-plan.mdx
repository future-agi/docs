Every Cloud organization starts on Free, where calls stop when a meter's allowance runs out. Move to Pay-as-you-go to keep those calls running at the usage rate, or add a paid add-on if the constraint is a plan limit rather than usage.

<Prerequisites title="Before you start">
- An organization [Owner or Admin role](/docs/roles-and-permissions) on Future AGI Cloud. [Self-hosted installations](/docs/self-hosting) are licensed separately.
- A default payment card for an add-on. Add-ons require Pay-as-you-go first.
</Prerequisites>

## Upgrade to Pay-as-you-go

1. Open **Settings > Plans & Pricing**.
2. Select **Pay-as-you-go**.
3. Confirm the change.

   Card details are collected on a secure checkout page hosted by the payment provider, so Future AGI never sees the card number.

4. Check which tier is marked **Current** on the Plans page.

   Pay-as-you-go should now be current. The platform fee is still $0, and your [free allowances](/docs/billing/concepts/what-you-are-billed-for#your-free-allowance) haven't changed; usage beyond them is billed instead of refused.

## Choose an add-on and billing interval

Boost, Scale and Enterprise sit on top of Pay-as-you-go rather than replacing it.

<div class="sdk-ref-table max-sm:[&_td]:px-1.5! max-sm:[&_th]:px-1.5!">

| Add-on | Platform fee | What it raises |
|--------|--------------|----------------|
| Boost | $250.00 / month | Higher counted limits, 500 API requests per minute, 90 day retention |
| Scale | $750.00 / month | Unlimited monitors, alerts, queues, shadow experiments and automation rules, concurrency 50, 2,000 API requests per minute, 365 day retention |
| Enterprise | $2,000.00 / month | Unlimited limits and rate, 7 year retention |

</div>

Add-ons bill monthly or annually, and the annual price carries a discount.

<div class="sdk-ref-table max-sm:[&_td]:px-1.5! max-sm:[&_th]:px-1.5!">

| Add-on | Monthly | Annual | Works out at |
|--------|---------|--------|--------------|
| Boost | $250.00 | $2,700 | $225.00 / month, 10% off |
| Scale | $750.00 | $7,200 | $600.00 / month, 20% off |
| Enterprise | $2,000.00 | $19,200 | $1,600.00 / month, 20% off |

</div>

For the exact limits behind each row, see [Plans and pricing](/docs/billing/reference/pricing).

1. Check that Pay-as-you-go is current.
2. Check that the organization has a default card under **Settings > Billing**.

   Without the card the add-on is rejected before checkout; a Free organization must upgrade first.

3. On **Plans & Pricing**, set **Annual billing** for an annual platform fee, or keep monthly billing.

   The interval applies to the platform fee only. Usage is always billed in the period it happened in.

4. Select the add-on you need.
5. Confirm the change.
6. Check which add-on is marked **Active**.

   Moving from Boost to Scale changes the add-on you already have; it does not stack a second one on top.

## Remove or reinstate an add-on

1. Find the active add-on on **Plans & Pricing**.
2. Select **Remove add-on**.
3. Check the end date beside the pending cancellation.

   The higher limits stay active until the end of the current billing period, not just until you select **Remove add-on**.

4. Compare your current resource counts with the Pay-as-you-go limits before that date.

   Your limits fall to the Pay-as-you-go level on the end date. Anything you are over by then, such as monitors beyond the lower cap, needs sorting out before the date arrives.

To keep the higher limits, reinstate the add-on **before** the scheduled date. This cancels the scheduled end without changing the bill or dropping the limits.

## Downgrade to Free

Free is hard-capped: when a meter's allowance is spent, calls on that meter stop until the next billing period.

1. Open **Settings > Plans & Pricing**.
2. Select **Switch to Free**.
3. Confirm the change.
4. Check the current tier on the Plans page before relying on Free's limits.

## Update payment details

1. Open **Settings > Billing**.
2. Find the card by brand, last four digits and expiry under **Payment methods**.
3. Select the card you want charged by default.

You can also add a card or remove an unused one. You can only manage your organization's cards, and you cannot remove the only card on a paid plan.

Edit the billing contact on the billing details page to change the invoice name, email, company, address, city, state, country or postal code. The link to the payment provider's portal lets you manage cards and receipts there instead.

## If a payment fails

A failed payment does not cut you off. The organization first enters a grace period. Usage continues while you receive a failure email, then a retry reminder and a final warning if the balance stays unpaid. After that, billable usage is refused until a payment succeeds. Access resumes as soon as payment succeeds; retries and receipts come from the payment provider.
