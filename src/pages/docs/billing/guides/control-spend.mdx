Billing on Future AGI is usage-based, so the bill follows your traffic, and traffic changes without anyone deciding it should. A quiet week and a load test land on the same invoice at very different totals.

When usage jumps, find the meter and workspace behind it before you change a budget. A **Notify** budget can tell you when usage crosses your threshold without stopping [trace ingestion](/docs/observe), [gateway calls](/docs/command-center) or [simulation runs](/docs/environments).

<Prerequisites title="Before you start">
- An organization on Cloud or Enterprise, where billing is available.
- [Owner or Admin permissions](/docs/roles-and-permissions) to see organization-wide usage and change budgets; other users can view usage for their workspaces.
- At least one meter with usage if you want to investigate a spike.
</Prerequisites>

## Find the meter that moved

1. Open **Settings > Usage Summary**.
2. Select the billing period you want to inspect.

   Above the cards you'll see the plan, platform fee, period dates and total estimated cost.

<img src="/images/docs/billing/guides/usage-overview.png" alt="Usage Summary page with the period total, the stat cards, and per-meter cards showing usage against the free allowance with a projection" style={{ borderRadius: '5px' }} />

*In this period every meter is within its free allowance, so the estimated total is just the platform fee.*

3. Open the meter with the unexpected usage.

   Each card shows usage so far, the free allowance and percentage used, projected period total, and estimated cost with its pricing bands.

4. Compare the trend with the preceding days or months.

   A single month shows daily usage; a range of months shows monthly totals. This view answers whether a jump was one bad day or a new baseline. A single spike that falls back to the old line is usually a backfill or a test run. A step that holds is a change in how much traffic your agents are doing, and that is the one worth a budget.

5. Break the meter down by workspace for the period.

   This compares workspaces for one meter, so you see which team is driving the number. The workspace filter on the overview instead scopes the entire page to one workspace.

   Do this before you set a budget. A ceiling on total spend catches everything but tells you nothing, while knowing which workspace is driving a meter tells you which meter is worth a ceiling.

The seven meters are storage, [credits](/docs/billing/concepts/what-you-are-billed-for), gateway requests, [gateway cache hits](/docs/command-center/features/caching), text simulation tokens, voice simulation minutes and tracing events. Read a projection as a pace, not a promise. It extrapolates from how fast the meter has moved so far, so it shifts as the month goes on and is least reliable in the first few days, when a single busy afternoon can drag the whole line upward.

## Preview the bill

1. Open **Settings > Billing**.
2. Compare the platform fee with the usage lines.

   The preview shows each meter above its allowance, its tier bands, any credits applied, the subtotal and the total. Viewing it doesn't charge you or consume a credit.

The preview is the same calculation the real invoice runs, stopped short of billing you. An invoice carries this period's platform fee in advance together with last period's usage in arrears, so the fee and usage lines cover different windows. [What lands on an invoice](/docs/billing/concepts/what-you-are-billed-for#what-lands-on-an-invoice) walks through the split.

## Set a budget that notifies without pausing

A budget is a threshold and an action. For a trial, use **Notify**, not **Pause**.

1. Open **Settings > Billing**, which holds your budgets.
2. Choose the meter from your workspace breakdown, or select total spend instead.
3. Enter a threshold in the meter's units (for example GB, credits, minutes or events), or in dollars for total spend.
4. Select **Notify**.
5. Add any extra email recipients for this budget.
6. If you want a Slack message, add this budget's Slack webhook.

   Each budget has its own recipients and webhook, so a storage budget can page the platform team while a simulation budget goes to whoever runs the test suite.

7. Save the budget.

| Action | What happens at the threshold |
| --- | --- |
| Notify | Sends an email and, if configured, a Slack message. |
| Warn | Sends the same notifications and shows a dismissible in-app banner. |
| Pause | Blocks further usage on the scoped meter and shows a banner you cannot dismiss. |

Both banners link to the budget that raised them. You can edit or delete a budget like any other setting.

<Warning>
Pause stops the work, not just the spend. Traffic on the paused meter is refused for the rest of the period, which means ingestion, gateway calls or simulation runs stop landing depending on what you scoped it to. Point it at a meter you can afford to lose until the period rolls over.
</Warning>

## If a notification hasn't arrived

1. Compare **actual** usage for the period with the threshold, not the projected period total.
2. Check the email recipients on the budget that applies to the meter.
3. For a missing Slack message, check the webhook on that same budget.

   Each budget fires at most once per billing period. Continuing past the same threshold doesn't send another alert; the budget is armed again in the next period.

A threshold email is distinct from the other billing messages: payment failure, payment retry reminder, final warning before downgrade, downgrade to Free, invoice generated, and invoice paid.

## Check the final charge

1. Open **Invoice History** in **Settings > Billing**.
2. Select the invoice for the period in question.

   History lists the period, plan, platform fee, usage total, credits applied, subtotal, tax, total and status. Inside the invoice, compare quantity, unit, unit price and amount; usage lines include their tier bands. Each invoice links to a hosted copy and a PDF.
