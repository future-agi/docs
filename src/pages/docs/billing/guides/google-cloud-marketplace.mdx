## About

If your team buys Future AGI Cloud through Google Cloud Marketplace, Google bills your Cloud Billing account. Start registration from the Marketplace purchase so Future AGI can link the organization you will own to the subscription your team paid for. For direct card checkout instead, see [Manage your plan](/docs/billing/guides/manage-your-plan).

## Register the organization owner

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your offer in Google Cloud Marketplace">
    Suppose you manage the support agent at Ridgeline Outfitters and your team has a Future AGI offer. In [Google Cloud Marketplace](https://console.cloud.google.com/marketplace/browse), review the plan, billing interval, and usage rates on that offer before you accept it. If your team has already accepted, open the existing purchase rather than placing another order.

    {/* MEDIA: A test Marketplace offer showing its plan and billing terms and where to open registration after purchase. */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete registration from the purchase">
    Follow the sign-up link from Marketplace. On **Create an account**, enter your **Full Name** and **Business Email ID**, then select **Continue** and check that inbox for sign-in instructions. Alternatively, select **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Github** on that same page. Use a work email that you have not already registered with Future AGI.

    You have one hour to submit the form after Marketplace sends you to Future AGI. Completing this step makes you the owner of the organization linked to Ridgeline’s purchase. Opening the form alone does not finish registration.

    {/* MEDIA: The Future AGI registration form reached from Marketplace, showing the name and business email fields and the alternative sign-in buttons. */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Return to the same subscription">
    Open Future AGI from the same Marketplace purchase again. Once the organization has an owner, the link takes you to sign-in instead of registration. Use the account you just created. The purchased plan takes effect when Google marks the subscription active.

    {/* MEDIA: A return visit from the same test purchase landing on Future AGI sign-in rather than the registration form. */}
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check what Google bills

Open **Settings > Usage Summary** in Future AGI to see the quantities your organization has used. For Marketplace billing, Future AGI reports credits, storage, gateway requests, gateway cache hits, text simulation tokens, and voice simulation minutes. It does not report tracing events as a Marketplace charge.

For example, if Ridgeline has already used its monthly gateway request allowance, the next 200 requests add 200 billable requests, not the entire month’s traffic. Future AGI subtracts each meter’s allowance and reports only the additional billable units. Google applies your offer’s prices, including any private offer terms. Future AGI does not also charge a card for that Marketplace usage.

## Renew, change, or cancel

Manage the subscription in Google Cloud Marketplace. In Future AGI, **Settings > Plans & Pricing** shows a notice instead of the controls for changing tiers or add-ons.

| Action in Marketplace | What happens in Future AGI |
| --- | --- |
| Renew the subscription | The organization continues on the renewed plan. You do not register again. |
| Request a plan change | You keep your current access until the change takes effect in Google. Future AGI then applies the new plan. |
| Cancel the subscription | A pending cancellation does not cut off access. When cancellation takes effect, Future AGI reports any remaining billable usage up to cancellation. If this was your only active subscription, the organization returns to Free. Cancellation does not delete your data. |

## If registration does not finish

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| `Invalid or expired GCP Marketplace token` | Future AGI could not verify the handoff from Google. Start again from the purchase in Marketplace, not from a saved URL. |
| `Invalid or expired onboarding token` | Close the old registration tab. Open sign-up again from the same Marketplace purchase to get a fresh one-hour session. You do not need to buy another subscription. |
| `An account with this email already exists` | The registration flow cannot attach the purchase to a different, existing Future AGI account. Use an unused work email for the new organization’s owner. |
| `This Marketplace subscription already has an account` | Return to the purchase and open Future AGI again. Sign in with the registered owner account instead of submitting the form a second time. |
