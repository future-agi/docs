---
title: "Debug failures"
description: "Get a diagnosis of a finished run: the goals your agent broke, the ways each one broke, and the calls behind them"
---

A run with failing calls leaves you a table to read one call at a time. **Debug failures** reads every call in the run for you and reports which goals your agent broke, the different ways each one broke, and which calls to open for each.

<Note>
You need a run that has finished; [Run a simulation](/docs/environments/guides/run-a-simulation) covers starting one. **Debug failures** stays disabled while a run is still playing calls.
</Note>

## Open the diagnosis

Open the [environment](/docs/environments/concepts/understanding-environments), go to **Runs** and click a finished run. Then click **Debug failures** in the run's header.

<img src="/images/docs/environments/guides/debug-failures/debug-failures-button.png" alt="The results page of Run 2 on the Northwind Pharmacy refill line environment, with the run's reliability stats, Run evals, Export and Debug failures in the header above the Test runs tab and its Failed 3 and Passed 5 chips" style={{ borderRadius: '5px' }} />
*The diagnosis covers the whole run, whatever the table is filtered to*

A drawer opens on the right and starts reading the run. While it works it shows a short status, such as `Reading transcripts…`, and you don't have to wait in front of it: close the drawer and keep working in the table. The diagnosis stays with the run, so the next time you click **Debug failures** on this run it opens with the result.

If the reading runs past four minutes, the status changes to `This is taking longer than expected. You can close this and check back.`

## Read the summary

The drawer's header counts the run, such as `3 failing of 8 measured`. **Measured** leaves out calls that errored before they could be graded, the same calls the **Error** chip counts on **Test runs**. **Failing** is the number of measured calls that broke at least one goal.

Under the tabs, one sentence sums the run up: `Your agent broke 2 goals on 3 of 8 calls, plus 1 one-off issue.` When nothing broke it reads `Your agent held every goal on 8 calls.`

A **goal** in this drawer is a [sub-goal](/docs/environments/concepts/scenarios-and-trials): one of the checks a scenario's calls are graded on, such as `checks_refill_status`. Each call on **Test runs** lists its sub-goals under the scenario name, with a tick or a cross.

## Read a goal card

Each goal your agent broke gets a card, and the card with the most broken calls comes first.

| On the card | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| `Broke on 2 of 6 calls` | The calls that broke the goal, out of the calls that tested it. A sub-goal belongs to some scenarios and not others, so the second number is usually smaller than the run |
| `Passes when …` | The goal's pass condition, when it has one |
| **Ways it broke** | One row per way the goal broke, with the number of calls that broke it that way |

The rows under **Ways it broke** are what save you the reading. Calls that broke the goal the same way share a row, so two rows mean two different problems behind one failed check, and usually two fixes. In the example run, the patient identification goal broke on two calls in two ways: on one the agent `Reads back the original date of birth after the caller corrects it`, and on the other it `Shares the refill date before the caller confirms the date of birth`.

Rows are ordered by their count. A card with more than five ways shows the first three and folds the rest into a row such as `3 other ways`; click it to list them all. A **Not explained yet** row counts calls that broke the goal and have no explanation, so open those and read them yourself.

*One failed check can hide more than one problem*

### Issues outside the broken goals

Below the cards, **Also seen, 1 call each** lists problems the diagnosis noticed that none of the broken goals cover, such as the agent asking for a prescription number the caller already gave. These don't make a call failing: a call can hold every goal and still appear here.

## Open the calls behind a finding

Every count in the drawer is a number of calls, and the button beside it opens exactly those calls: **View** on a row, **View 2 calls** on a card, and **View call** on a one-off issue.

The drawer closes and **Test runs** shows only those calls, with their groups already open and a chip such as `2 affected calls` beside **Filter**. Click a row to open the call and read its transcript and verdicts. Dismiss the chip to get the whole run back.

<img src="/images/docs/environments/guides/debug-failures/affected-calls-test-runs.png" alt="The Test runs tab of Run 2 after clicking View 2 calls on a goal card, showing a 2 affected calls chip beside Filter and only the two authenticate-caller-dob-correction calls, Trial 2 and Trial 1, both Failed" style={{ borderRadius: '5px' }} />
*The filters you add on top keep the calls from the diagnosis until you dismiss the chip*

## When the diagnosis doesn't finish

A diagnosis that fails shows `Couldn't finish the diagnosis`, a reason, and a **Try again** button.

| Reason shown | Why | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| `Diagnosis is available once every call in this run finishes` | Some calls in the run haven't finished | Wait for the run to finish, then click **Try again** |
| `Couldn't read this run. Try again` | The run couldn't be read this time | Click **Try again** |

A finished diagnosis can also carry the line `Still grouping how each goal broke; the breakdown may change.` The goals and their counts are in, the rows under **Ways it broke** can still change, and the drawer updates on its own.

## Act on the diagnosis

The diagnosis tells you where to look; the fix happens outside the drawer. For each goal, open a call or two and confirm the problem in the transcript. Then decide whether the agent is wrong or the test is: [Read run results](/docs/environments/guides/read-run-results#act-on-what-you-find) covers how to tell the two apart, and a test that checks the wrong thing is fixed through [chat orchestration](/docs/environments/concepts/chat-orchestration).

After a fix, [re-run the calls that broke](/docs/environments/guides/read-run-results#re-run-selected-calls). **Re-run** offers two ways:

- **Re-run in this run** makes the calls again inside the same run, and their new results replace the old ones on **Test runs**
- **Run as a new simulation** records a new run with its own diagnosis; open it and click **Debug failures** there to see whether the goal still breaks

<Note>
Two parts of the drawer can't be used yet. The **Imagine** tab, for asking questions about the run and getting charts back, isn't connected yet, and **Run Self Improvement**, at the bottom of a finished diagnosis, is marked **BETA** and stays disabled.
</Note>
