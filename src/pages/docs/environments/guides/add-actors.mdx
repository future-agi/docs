title: "Add actors"

---

Shape the cast of an environment on its [Actors](/docs/environments/concepts/actors) tab: add, edit or remove actors and set their limits.

<Note>
You need an environment in **Ready** status; [Build an environment](/docs/environments/guides/build-an-environment) covers getting one. If the environment has no **Actors** tab, actors are not turned on for your deployment. See the [configuration reference](/docs/self-hosting/configuration/reference#agent-simulations-in-hosted-sandboxes).
</Note>

Every change on this tab goes to the draft. It becomes a version only when the environment's changes are saved. While you have unsaved actor changes, the tab shows **Unsaved changes: saved with the environment's other changes**.

## Check the cast

Open the environment and go to **Actors**, the third tab, right after **Contract**. Each row is one actor, grouped under **On the caller's side** or **In the world**; check who is in each group. **What they see** on a row shows what that actor can see. An environment with none reads **No actors yet. Add one, or ask the builder chat.**

<img src="/images/docs/environments/guides/add-actors/actors-tab-cast.png" alt="The Actors tab of a deals desk environment, with two actors on the side of the caller and three actors in the world, each row showing what they may do, when they come in and What they see" style={{ borderRadius: '5px' }} />
*Actors sit on the caller's side or in the world*

If **Needs your decision** appears, choose **Accept as written** to keep the cast as it is, or **Edit actor** to change the actor concerned.

Environments seeded from a template are locked: you can read the cast, but add, edit and remove are hidden and **Limits** is disabled.

Where the cast is listed read-only, it shows as **Actors (n)**.

To see which actors a scenario includes, open the scenario's details and read its **Actors** row.

## Add an actor

1. Click **Add actor** and pick **On the caller's side** or **In the world**.
2. Fill in **Name**, optionally a **Role**, and the **Id**. **Brought in by** is optional
3. Under **How they join the conversation**, choose **Heard nearby**, **Takes the phone**, **Phone menu** or **Writes in the chat**. Choose **Doesn't speak in the conversation** if the actor should act only through the world's actions
4. Describe what the actor wants and may do. **What they want**, **May agree to**, **Keeps to themselves**, **Hands over when**, **Is done when**, **What they know** and **How they behave** all shape how they speak and decide
5. Under **How they act in the world**, choose how the actor acts, then pick **World actions they may take**. If a chosen action already belongs to another actor, an alert names the action and its current holder and says giving it here lets both take it. Tick **Both should have it** to let both actors take it
6. Set **Comes in** to say when the actor first takes part
7. Set **Waits, in seconds (optional)** to how long the world waits for the actor (0 waits as long as the call lasts), and **If they do not answer** to what happens then
8. Give it material to draw on: **Files they can read** and **Records they can read**
9. Click **Save to draft**. **Cancel** closes the form without saving

<img src="/images/docs/environments/guides/add-actors/add-actor-form.png" alt="The Add actor form for an actor in the world, with Name Dana Brooks, Id dana_brooks, Role store manager who approves refunds and How they join the conversation set so the actor never speaks in the conversation" style={{ borderRadius: '5px' }} />
*Name the actor and pick how they join, then click Save to draft*

For a voice actor, **How they sound** and **How loud next to the caller** set their voice. **Model they are played on**, **Most turns/lines in a call** and **More details** are further down the form. **Files they can read** also has **Upload a document**; an upload never replaces a file that already exists. If an actor is too long, the form tells you.

## Edit or remove an actor

Click **Edit** on a row to open the form titled **Edit** followed by the actor's name. Click **Remove**, then confirm **Remove** followed by the name. The prompt reads **They leave the draft. Saved versions keep them.**

While a scenario includes an actor, you cannot remove it, and you cannot change how it joins the conversation.

## Set the limits

Click **Limits** to open them, and **Hide limits** to close them. Click **Save limits to draft** when done.

| Limit | Default |
| --- | --- |
| How many at once on a call | 2 |
| Caller takes back after agent turns | 4 |
| Seconds of actors per call | 60 |
| Most messages in a conversation before the run | 20 |
| Most turns each actor takes during a run | 10 |
| Most turns all actors take together in a run | 20 |

The first three apply to voice calls. Chat has no limit for them, so do not rely on them there.

## Check who can reach whom

With two or more actors, **Who can reach whom** appears. Read it after a change to check the cast still fits together.
