---
title: "Import evidence"
description: "Turn Observe sessions and calls, dataset rows or transcripts into reviewed scenarios, then run an epoch on them"
---

The conversations that went wrong with real callers make the best tests. **Import evidence** turns them into scenarios in your environment, and you review each one before it joins the suite. Once they're in, an epoch can fix the failures they show.

<Note>
Evidence from production becomes a **regression test**. A transcript or dataset row you don't mark as production, or a conversation from a Simulator project, becomes a plain test instead. You choose when you import, and it can't change afterwards.
</Note>

## Import from Observe

Open the Observe project and go to its [sessions](/docs/observe/concepts/sessions), or to its voice calls for a voice agent. Tick the ones you want, up to 20 at a time. Tick them one by one: **Select all** isn't available for this.

Click **Create environment test** in the bar above the list, choose the **Environment** to add them to, and click **Create environment test** again. Nothing runs yet, and nothing joins the suite until you add it.

<img src="/images/docs/environments/guides/import-evidence/observe-create-environment-test.png" alt="An Observe project's Sessions list with three sessions ticked and Create environment test in the selection bar above the list" style={{ borderRadius: '5px' }} />
*Tick the sessions where your agent went wrong, or where it got something right that it must keep doing*

The environment opens on its **Scenarios** tab, with the import ready to review.

## Import dataset rows

Open the [dataset](/docs/dataset), tick the rows, up to 20 at a time, and click **Create environment test**. Then tell it how to read the rows:

- **Conversation column**: the column that holds the conversation, as chat messages in JSON or as lines that start `User:` or `Agent:`. Audio-only rows aren't supported yet
- **Label columns (optional)**: up to 10 columns read as context, such as the caller's intent
- **Expected outcome column (optional)**: what should have happened. The scenario's expected behavior is written from it, and the environment's rules win if the two disagree
- **These rows come from production**: tick it for rows from real conversations, to make regression tests

Choose the **Environment** and click **Create environment test**. The rows are read again from the dataset each time you review the import.

## Add a transcript

On the environment's **Scenarios** tab, click **Import evidence**, then **Add a transcript**. Paste a transcript, or click **Upload files** to add up to 20 files.

Pick the **Format**: **JSON messages**, **User: / Agent: lines**, or **CSV with role and text**. Name the transcript, tick **These transcripts come from production** if they do, and click **Create environment test**.

Emails, phone numbers, and card and ID numbers become placeholders before the text is stored. An import keeps up to 60,000 characters, shared by its files.

**Import evidence** also has **Open Observe** and **Open a dataset**, which take you to the other two starting points.

## Review the imported cases

The **Review imported evidence** drawer opens on the import, such as `3 sessions from Observe`. Each source is one **case**, and the drawer takes you through four steps:

1. **Evidence**: a card for each case, with what the record holds and what it's missing, and a link back to its source. A case the environment already holds shows **Already imported**, with a link to the existing case. The drawer also says how many values of personal data become placeholders before anything becomes scenario text
2. **Starting state**: the records your agent's tools read first, such as an earlier refund, aren't in a conversation. The draft marks them synthetic, so the test isn't an exact replay
3. **Drafted scenarios**: for each case, choose what its scenario should be, such as **Reproduce the failure as a regression test** or **Keep this success as a regression test**. Under each choice, the card says what the scenario keeps from the evidence and what it adds. **Set every waiting case to** picks one choice for all of them. Then click the draft button, such as **Draft 3 scenarios**
4. **Add to the suite**: click the save button, such as **Save 3 to the suite**. If the environment has unsaved changes, it reads **Add 3 to unsaved changes** instead; press **Save** in the draft bar to make them live

<img src="/images/docs/environments/guides/import-evidence/review-imported-evidence.png" alt="The Review imported evidence drawer for 3 sessions from Observe, showing the Evidence step with a card for each session, the line about personal data placeholders, and the Drafted scenarios step with a choice of Reproduce the failure as a regression test for each case" style={{ borderRadius: '5px' }} />
*You decide what each case tests; the builder writes the scenario from that*

Drafting takes up to 90 minutes, and you can close the drawer while it runs. Don't edit the environment until it ends: an edit made meanwhile blocks the drafted cases.

## Pick up an import later

Imports you haven't finished stay under **Draft imports** on the **Scenarios** tab, each with its source and where it got to, such as `drafted, not added` or `blocked: import again to retry`. Click **Resume** to open its review again, or **Discard** to drop it. Discarding never removes cases already added to the suite.

A case is blocked when it can't be drafted or added, for example because the environment changed after it was drafted. Import its source again; the new import replaces the blocked case.

## Run Epoch on the imported failures

Once the cases are in the suite, [run the environment](/docs/environments/guides/run-a-simulation). When the run finishes and some of its failing scenarios came from imported evidence, **Run Epoch** shows in the run's header, next to **Debug failures**.

<img src="/images/docs/environments/guides/import-evidence/run-epoch-imported.png" alt="The header of Run 5 of the Northwind Pharmacy refill line with Run Epoch next to Debug failures, and its tooltip saying it starts an epoch from the 3 failing scenarios that came from imported evidence" style={{ borderRadius: '5px' }} />
*Run Epoch here starts from the failures your imported cases showed*

The launch form starts with the imported failing scenarios as the cases to fix. If there are too few, it adds more failing scenarios from the run to reach the minimum, and says how many of each it picked. [Run Epoch](/docs/environments/guides/run-epoch#pick-the-cases-to-fix-and-protect) covers the rest of the form. When you ship the winner as a pull request, its description says where the cases came from.
