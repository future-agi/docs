## About

Settings is where you manage your Future AGI account: your profile, organization configuration, team members, workspaces, AI provider connections, external integrations, usage tracking, and billing. Open it from the workspace switcher at the top of the sidebar. API keys are not here, they live under **Build > Keys** in the left sidebar.

Access to different settings depends on your role. See [Roles & Permissions](/docs/roles-and-permissions) for details.
