## About

Workspaces let you organize projects and control access within your organization. Each workspace has its own datasets, prompts, members, AI provider configurations, integrations, and usage tracking. Use workspaces to separate environments (production vs staging), teams (engineering vs data science), or projects.

Access: **Owner** and **Admin** create workspaces. A Workspace Admin manages the workspaces they administer. See [Roles & Permissions](/docs/roles-and-permissions).

## Creating a workspace

There are two ways in.

**From Settings.** Go to **Settings > Workspaces** and click **Create New Workspace**.

**From the switcher.** Open the switcher at the top of the sidebar, hover the workspace name, and choose **Create Workspace**.

Both open the same dialog. Enter a name and click **Create workspace**. You become the admin of the workspace you create.

<Note>
Workspace names are unique inside an organization. Reusing one gives you **Workspace with this name already exists**.
</Note>

If **Create Workspace** is not offered in your switcher, your role cannot create one. Ask an Owner or Admin.

## The workspaces list

**Settings > Workspaces** lists every workspace in the organization with its name, admins, start date, and last updated date, and has a search box above the table.

<Note>
There is no way to delete a workspace from the dashboard. Neither this list nor a workspace's own General page offers one. If you need a workspace removed, contact the Future AGI team.
</Note>

## Workspace settings

Open a workspace from **Your Workspaces** in the Settings navigation, or from **Workspace settings** in the switcher. What you get depends on your role in that workspace.

| Tab | What it controls | Who sees it |
|---|---|---|
| General | Workspace name | Everyone in the workspace |
| Members | Who has access and their workspace role | Workspace Admin, and Owners and Admins |
| Integrations | Workspace-specific integration connections | Workspace Admin, and Owners and Admins |
| AI Providers | Workspace-specific AI provider configurations | Workspace Admin, and Owners and Admins |

A Workspace Viewer gets the General tab with no save control, so the page is read only for them.

## Managing workspace members

1. Open the workspace settings
2. Go to the **Members** tab
3. The grid lists each person with their user name, organization role, workspace role, email, status, and start date
4. Add or remove members, and set their workspace role: Workspace Admin, Workspace Member, or Workspace Viewer

A Workspace Admin can also invite straight into their workspace with **Invite members** in the switcher. That invite grants a workspace role only, never an organization role.

<Note>
Organization roles and workspace roles are separate. A user can be a Member at the org level but a Workspace Admin in a specific workspace, and an organization Viewer added as a Workspace Member can create and edit there. See [Roles & Permissions](/docs/roles-and-permissions) for how these interact.
</Note>
