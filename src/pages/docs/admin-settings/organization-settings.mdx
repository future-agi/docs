## About

Organization Settings lets you manage your organization's name and security policies. Changes here affect all members of the organization.

Access: **Owner** and **Admin** only.

## Settings

| Setting | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Organization Name | The display name for your organization. Visible across the platform. |
| Require 2FA for all members | Require two-factor authentication across the organization. When enabled, all organization members must set up two-factor authentication. |

## How to

### Change Organization Name

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open organization settings">
    Go to **Settings > Org Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the name">
    Edit the organization name field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Enforce 2FA for All Members

<Warning>
Turn on two-factor for your own account first. Until you do, the organization toggle is disabled and the page shows a link to Profile.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open organization settings">
    Go to **Settings > Org Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable the policy">
    Under **Organization Security**, turn on **Require 2FA for all members**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Members enrol">
    Members without 2FA are prompted to set it up at their next login.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Enforcing 2FA is recommended for organizations handling sensitive data or operating in regulated industries.
</Note>
