## About

Organization Settings lets you manage your organization's name and security policies. Changes here affect all members of the organization.

Access: **Owner** and **Admin** only.

## Settings

| Setting | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Organization Name | The display name for your organization. Visible across the platform. |
| Two-Factor Policy | Require 2FA across the organization. Turning it on starts a grace period you choose, so existing members have time to enrol rather than being locked out. |

## How to

### Change Organization Name

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open organization settings">
    Go to **Settings > Org Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the name">
    Edit the organization name field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Enforce 2FA for All Members

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open organization settings">
    Go to **Settings > Org Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable 2FA on your own account first">
    You cannot require it for the organization until two-factor is enabled on your own account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable the policy">
    In the Two-Factor Policy section, enable the enforcement toggle and set the grace period.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Members enrol">
    Members without 2FA are prompted at their next login, and have until the grace period ends to enrol.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Enforcing 2FA is recommended for organizations handling sensitive data or operating in regulated industries.
</Note>
