Two-factor authentication (2FA) adds a second verification step when you log in. If you lose the device that holds your authenticator app, recovery codes are the way back in, so save them as soon as you enable 2FA. This guide enables 2FA, stores the recovery codes, and registers a passkey so you can log in without a password.

<Prerequisites title="Before you start">
- All [organization roles](/docs/roles-and-permissions) can manage their own profile and sign-in settings.
- Use a password or social login to change these settings. A [SAML session](/docs/release-notes) cannot change two-factor, passkey or recovery-code settings, or reset a password.
- For 2FA, have an app that generates time-based one-time passwords (TOTP), such as Google Authenticator, Authy or 1Password.
- Choose a secure place to store recovery codes separately from your authenticator device.
</Prerequisites>

## Enroll an authenticator

1. Open **Settings > Profile**.
2. In the Security section, click **Enable Two-Factor Authentication**.
3. Scan the QR code with your authenticator app.
4. Enter the 6-digit code from the app to confirm.

   2FA is now on. Recovery codes are available only after you enable it.

## Save a way back in

1. In the Security section, click **View Recovery Codes**.
2. Store the codes in a secure location, separate from your authenticator device.

Recovery codes are backup codes you can use if you lose access to your 2FA device. If you lose both the device and the codes, you will need to contact support.

## Replace recovery codes

1. Click **Regenerate**.

   This invalidates all previous codes.

2. Replace your stored copy with the new codes.

## Register a passkey

A passkey lets you log in without a password, using biometrics (fingerprint or face) or a hardware security key.

1. In the Security section, click **Add Passkey**.
2. Follow your browser's prompts.

You can register multiple passkeys, for example one on your laptop and one on your phone. To remove a passkey, click the delete icon next to it.

## Disable 2FA

To disable 2FA, verify with either your current TOTP code or a recovery code.

## Change profile details

The same page holds your personal account details.

| Field | What you can change |
| --- | --- |
| Full Name | Click the name to edit it in a dialog. |
| Email | The address is set at account creation and cannot be edited here. |
| Password | Click **Reset Password**. Resets are limited to once per hour. |
