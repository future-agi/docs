---
title: "Guardrail"
description: "What a guardrail span captures: a safety or policy check and its verdict."
---

A guardrail span records a safety or policy check on an input or output. It carries what was checked and the verdict it returned.

For the full set of keys any span carries, see [Span attributes](/docs/observe/reference/span-attributes). For the other kinds, see [Span types](/docs/observe/reference/span-types).
