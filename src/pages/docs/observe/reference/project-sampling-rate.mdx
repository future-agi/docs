---
title: "Project sampling rate"
description: "Control what fraction of a project's traces Observe keeps, to manage volume and cost."
---

The sampling rate controls what fraction of a project's traces Observe keeps. Keeping everything is fine at low volume; at high throughput, sampling down keeps storage and cost in check while still giving you a representative view of what your app is doing.

This reference covers how to set a project's sampling rate, the defaults, and how sampling interacts with sessions, evals, and alerts.
