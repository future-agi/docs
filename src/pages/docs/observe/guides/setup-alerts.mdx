---
title: "Setup alerts"
description: "Get notified when your project degrades, so you hear about problems before your users do."
---

Alerts watch your traces and metrics and notify you when something crosses a threshold you set, like a spike in errors, a jump in latency, or a failing eval. This guide adds alerts to the `self-improving-agent` project from the [quickstart](/docs/observe/quickstart).

## In this guide

- Choose what to watch: error rate, latency, cost, or an eval score
- Set the threshold and the time window that trigger the alert
- Pick where the alert is delivered
- Confirm the alert fires, and review its history
