---
title: "Trace drawers"
description: "Open a trace to inspect its spans, inputs, outputs, and timing in the detail drawer."
---

Click a row in the trace table and the trace drawer opens: the span tree on one side, and each span's input, output, timing, and attributes on the other. This is where you read what actually happened in a request, using the `self-improving-agent` project from the [quickstart](/docs/observe/quickstart).

## In this guide

- Open a trace and read its span tree
- Inspect a span's input, output, and attributes
- Follow timing and status down the tree
