---
title: "Share a trace"
description: "Create a trace link, choose restricted or public access, and invite teammates by email"
---

Share a [trace](/docs/observe/concepts/traces) by sending someone a link. Choose **Restricted** to limit access to invited accounts, or **Anyone with the link** to let people open it without signing in.

## Create a link

1. Open a project in **Observe**, then click a trace to open its drawer.
2. Click the share icon in the drawer header, with the tooltip **Share trace**.
3. The **Share** dialog checks for existing links. If that check succeeds and finds none, it creates a **Restricted** link. A failed check does not create a new link.
4. Choose who can open the link using the access options below, then click **Copy** and send the URL to your recipient.

## Choose who can open it

Under **Who can access**, choose one of these modes:

| Mode | Who can open the link |
|---|---|
| **Restricted** | A signed-in Future AGI user whose account email is invited, or the link's creator. Email matching ignores case. |
| **Anyone with the link** | Anyone who has the URL. No sign-in is required. |

<Warning>
A public link exposes the trace contents, including span inputs and outputs, to anyone who has the URL. Check the trace for customer data and other sensitive content before choosing **Anyone with the link**.
</Warning>

## Invite people to a restricted link

1. Keep **Restricted** selected.
2. Under **Invite people**, enter the recipient's account email and click **Invite**, or press Enter.
3. Click **Copy** and send them the link. They need to sign in to Future AGI with the invited email address to open it.

<Note>
Copying a restricted URL does not grant access. A signed-out recipient needs to sign in; a signed-in recipient with a different, uninvited email cannot open it. The link's creator also has access when signed in.
</Note>
