---
title: "Share outside your organization"
description: "Give somebody one dataset or one Observe project without giving them a role, and know which of the two you can take back."
---

A share link grants access to a single resource without granting a role, which makes it the fastest way to show a customer or an auditor one thing. It is also the one access path that ignores workspaces entirely, and only one of the two kinds can be withdrawn afterwards. This guide covers both, and which is which.

## Before you start

- You can create or edit in the workspace the resource lives in. For a Workspace Viewer the **Share** button is greyed out
- You have decided whether the person should be named, because a dataset link cannot be restricted to named people and an Observe project link can
- You accept that a link you send is effectively permanent, since nothing in the dashboard sets an expiry or issues a fresh URL

## Share a dataset

Open the dataset and click **Share**. The dialog gives you the dataset's own address, a copy button, **Cancel** and **Done**, and it says *Share this link to give others access to the selected dataset. Anyone with the link will be able to view the data.*

That is the whole feature. There is no restricted option, no expiry and no revoke, and once you click **Done** there is nothing in the dialog to undo it. Whoever holds the URL can open the dataset with no account and no sign-in.

<Warning>
**A dataset link cannot be taken back from the dashboard.** Contact the Future AGI team if one has to be withdrawn. Send one only where public access to that data is acceptable.
</Warning>

## Share an Observe project

Open the project and click **Share**. An Observe project behaves differently in three ways that matter.

It hands out a separate `app.futureagi.com/shared/...` address rather than the project's normal one. Its dialog reopens at any time with the link and the current setting. And under **Who can access** it carries two modes.

**Restricted** is the default, and only the people you add can open it. **Anyone with the link** drops the sign-in entirely.

A restricted share also has an **Invite people** field, so you can name the email addresses directly. Those people sign in, but they do not have to join your organization, which is what makes this the safer of the two paths for a customer who already has a Future AGI account.

## Narrow a share you already sent

Reopen **Share** on the project and set **Who can access** back to **Restricted**. Anyone not on its list stops being able to open it.

That is the only control the dashboard gives you over a link that is already out, and it exists for Observe projects only. Nothing anywhere issues a fresh URL or sets an expiry, so treat every link you send as permanent and share it deliberately.
