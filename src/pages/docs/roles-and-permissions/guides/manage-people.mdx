---
title: "Add, change and remove people"
description: "Invite someone, change what they reach, and take their access away without leaving a live credential behind."
---

Adding someone is the easy half. The half that goes wrong is taking access away, because removing a person from the organization ends their sign-in and leaves every API key they created working. This guide covers the whole lifecycle on **Settings > Members**, and what an offboarding has to include to actually close.

## Before you start

- You are an Owner or an Admin, the two roles that can open **Settings > Members**. A Workspace Admin manages their own workspace's members instead, from **Workspace settings** in the switcher
- You can act on anyone whose organization role is lower than yours. Owners are the exception and can act on other Owners
- Every Member and Viewer needs at least one workspace, so decide which before you start

## Invite someone

There are two ways in, and which one you get depends on your role.

**From the Members page.** Click **Invite User** at the top right. This is the Owner and Admin path, and it is the only one that carries an Organization Role field.

**From the workspace switcher.** Choose **Invite members**. This is the Workspace Admin path, and it appears only while they are standing in a workspace they administer. Once the dialog is open the workspaces picker offers every workspace they belong to, so someone who is Workspace Admin of one workspace and a Workspace Member of another can still invite into both, as long as they start from the one they administer.

Either way, one invite can cover several addresses as a comma separated list, and every address in it gets the same organization role, the same workspace role and the same set of workspaces. Granting Admin or Owner removes both workspace fields and replaces them with *Owner and Admin roles have automatic access to all workspaces.*

<img src="/images/docs/rbac/invite-modal.png" alt="The invite dialog, showing the emails field, the organization role selector, one workspace role, and the workspaces to apply it to." style={{ borderRadius: '5px' }} />

*Granting Admin or Owner removes the workspace fields, because those roles reach every workspace already*

The new row shows as **Pending** until the person accepts, or **Active** immediately if the address already has a Future AGI account. A Pending row carries exactly two actions, **Resend the invite** and **Cancel invite**.

<Note>
A Workspace Admin's invite has no Organization Role field, so they cannot set anyone's organization role and only an Owner or Admin can change it afterwards. If your team leads onboard their own people, check **Settings > Members** afterwards and set the organization role yourself.
</Note>

An invite is valid for 7 days, after which it shows as Expired. The link inside the email expires sooner than the invite itself, so resend rather than waiting if the link stops working. Resending issues a fresh link and restarts the window.

Inviting someone who is already an active member does not create a second invite. It adds that person to the workspaces you selected. To change an existing member's organization role, use **Edit user info** on their row.

## Change someone's access

<img src="/images/docs/rbac/users-list.png" alt="The Members page, with one row per person showing their organization role, their workspaces, their status, and their start date." style={{ borderRadius: '5px' }} />

*Every person carries one organization role and the workspaces they belong to*

**Settings > Members** lists everyone with their name, organization role, workspaces, email, status, and start date, and filters for status and role. Each row carries **Edit user info** and **Remove from organization**.

Where you cannot act on someone, the menu is disabled and says why: *You can't edit your own role*, or *You cannot manage a user at or above your own level.*

**Edit user info** holds an Organization Role and a Workspace Role. Setting someone to Member or Viewer adds a required **Workspaces** field, because *Members and Viewers need explicit workspace access.* Any workspace you leave unselected is removed from them in the same step, so read that field before you save it.

- Promoting someone to Admin or Owner makes them a Workspace Admin in every workspace at once
- Demoting an Admin to Member or Viewer asks you to pick the workspaces they keep
- The last remaining Owner can neither be demoted nor removed. Promote a second Owner first

## Remove or reactivate someone

Removing someone from the organization asks you to confirm, then ends their sign-in and marks them Deactivated. Removing them from a single workspace leaves their organization membership intact.

A few things are blocked outright:

- You cannot remove yourself, from the organization or from a workspace. Ask another Owner or Admin
- You cannot remove anyone from their last workspace
- You cannot remove an Owner or Admin from a workspace, since they reach every workspace regardless
- You cannot remove the last remaining Owner

Removal does not delete anything a person produced. Annotations, scores and other output stay in place.

Reactivating is not something the dashboard offers. A removed person is marked Deactivated and no control anywhere brings them back, so contact the Future AGI team if somebody has to be restored.

## Require two-factor authentication

Owners and Admins reach [**Settings > Org Settings**](/docs/admin-settings/organization-settings), titled Organization Settings, which holds the organization name and the security policy.

Under **Organization Security** there is one toggle, **Require 2FA for all members**. With it on, all organization members must set up two-factor authentication.

<Note>
You must enable two-factor authentication on your own account before requiring it for the organization. The page links straight to Profile Settings so you can.
</Note>

Members and Viewers cannot open Org Settings, so they cannot change the policy.

## Close out an offboarding

<Warning>
**A platform API key belongs to the organization, not to the person who created it.** Removing someone does not disable any key they made, and anything still using that key keeps working.

Go through **Build > Keys** in **every workspace they belonged to**, because that page only ever shows the keys of the workspace you have open. Check the **Created By** column and disable theirs. Deleting needs an Owner, so if you are an Admin, disable it now and hand the deletion to an Owner.
</Warning>

Two more things survive a removal and are worth a look on the way out. Any [share link](/docs/roles-and-permissions/guides/share-outside-your-organization) they created still works, and a dataset link cannot be withdrawn from the dashboard at all. And there is no activity log anywhere in the product, so if you need a record of what they changed, it has to come from Future AGI rather than from a screen.
