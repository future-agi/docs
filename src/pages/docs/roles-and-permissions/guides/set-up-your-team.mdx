title: "Set up your team"
description: "Decide your workspaces, pick a role for each person, invite them, and hand out keys that land in the right place."
---

An organization arrives with one workspace and one person in it, and the shape you give it in the first hour is the shape it keeps, because a workspace cannot be deleted from the dashboard and a key cannot be moved between workspaces. This guide covers the five decisions that are awkward to undo, in the order they come up.

## Before you start

- You are the Owner, or an Admin, the two roles that can create workspaces and open **Settings > Members**
- You know which groups of people must not see each other's work, since that is the only question a workspace answers
- You have the email address of everyone you are inviting

## Decide how many workspaces you need

A workspace is the data boundary. Your datasets, traces, evaluations, prompts, annotations and knowledge bases live inside one workspace and cannot be seen from another. [What is scoped where](/docs/roles-and-permissions/concepts/organizations-and-workspaces#what-is-scoped-where) has the full list, along with the four things the boundary does not cover.

One workspace is enough if everyone is allowed to see everything. Add a second when one group must not see another group's work, or when you want production and staging kept apart.

Your organization already has one, called **Default Workspace**. Nothing in the dashboard deletes a workspace, this one included, so use it for one of your teams, leave it empty, or ask the Future AGI team to remove it.

There are two ways to add one, and both open the same dialog. Either open the **workspace switcher** at the top of the left sidebar, hover the workspace name and choose **Create Workspace**, or go to **Settings > Workspaces** and click **Create New Workspace**. Name it and click **Create workspace**. You become its admin. Names must be unique in the organization, and reusing one gives you **Workspace with this name already exists**.

<Note>
The switcher does more than switch. It creates workspaces, moves you between workspaces and organizations, opens Settings, and is where a Workspace Admin invites people. You will be back in it throughout this guide.
</Note>

## Pick a role for each person

Everyone gets two roles. The organization role decides who they can manage and whether they reach organization settings and billing; the workspace role decides what they can do with the work. [How the two roles combine](/docs/roles-and-permissions/concepts/how-roles-combine) covers the interaction; this is the shortlist.

| What they need to do | Organization role | Workspace role |
|---|---|---|
| Set the organization up | Owner | Every workspace, automatically |
| Share the account, the people and the billing with you | Admin | Every workspace, automatically |
| Run one workspace and manage the people in it | Member | Workspace Admin |
| Build and edit inside one workspace | Viewer | Workspace Member |
| Read one workspace without changing it | Viewer | Workspace Viewer |

<Warning>
**Owners and Admins reach every workspace, automatically and permanently.** You cannot make someone an Admin and keep one workspace out of their reach. If a boundary matters, make them a Member or Viewer and add them only to the workspaces they should see.
</Warning>

To give someone control of one workspace and nothing else, make them an organization **Member** and a **Workspace Admin** in that workspace. They can then invite into it and manage its members, without reaching billing, organization settings, or anyone else's workspace.

<Note>
**Why the Workspace Members above are organization Viewers, not Members.** A team lead can only manage people whose organization role is lower than their own. If the people they lead are also organization Members, the lead's row menu is disabled with *You cannot manage a user at or above your own level.*

Organization Viewer takes almost nothing away here. The **Workspace Member** role still creates, edits and shares everything in that workspace, and still opens the workspace's own Integrations and AI Providers tabs. What it drops is the organization-level **AI Providers** page in Settings.

If that page is needed, use Member instead, and the team lead will not be able to remove them.
</Note>

## Invite them

Go to **Settings > Members** and click **Invite User**. Enter the addresses in **Emails**, comma separated, so one invite can cover several people. Pick the **Organization Role**, then the **Workspace Role**, then the **Workspaces** it applies to, and click **Send Invite**.

One invite carries one organization role, one workspace role and one set of workspaces, and every address in it gets all three. People who need different roles or different workspaces need separate invites, so send one per group rather than one for the whole team.

Choosing Admin or Owner removes both workspace fields and says *Owner and Admin roles have automatic access to all workspaces.*

Each person appears as **Pending** until they accept, or immediately as **Active** if they already have a Future AGI account. An invite lasts 7 days, and a Pending row carries **Resend the invite** and **Cancel invite**.

## Hand out the API keys

Your SDK and your traces authenticate with a platform API key.

<Warning>
**Switch into the right workspace first.** A key belongs to whichever workspace you have open when you create it. Traces sent with it land there, and the Keys page only ever lists the keys of the workspace you are currently in. The dialog says none of this and gives you no workspace to pick, so the switcher is your only control over it.

Make the key for `research` while you are inside `research`. A key made in the wrong workspace does not appear on the Keys page until you switch back to it.
</Warning>

Open **Build > Keys** in the left sidebar and click **Add API Key**. It asks for a name and nothing else. You are then shown an API Key and a Secret Key, both masked, so take each with its copy button. If you are making this key for somebody else, send them both values now, before you close the dialog. It does not come back, and there is no way to reveal a secret later.

Only Owners, Admins and Workspace Admins can open this page, so decide who issues keys. [API Keys](/docs/admin-settings/api-keys) covers rotation and the difference between a platform key and a gateway key.

## Check it worked

Three read-only checks close the setup out.

- **Settings > Members** lists everyone with their role, their workspaces, and Active or Pending
- **Settings > Workspaces** lists every workspace with its admins
- Ask one teammate to sign in. They should see their own workspaces in the switcher and nothing else

The setup is done when every person is on the Members page with the role you intended, and a Member or Viewer signing in sees only the workspaces you put them in.
