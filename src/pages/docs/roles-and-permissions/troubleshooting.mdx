## In this page

Most access reports are one of three things: the person was never added to the workspace, their workspace role is read-only, or the page they want is not in their role's navigation. Work down the first table before raising anything. The second table covers the four cases where the dashboard genuinely cannot help and the Future AGI team has to.

## When someone cannot see something

| What they say | What to check |
|---|---|
| "I cannot see the workspace at all" | They were never added to it. Add them from **Settings > Members** |
| "I can see it but I cannot create anything" | They are a Workspace Viewer there. Change their workspace role |
| "I cannot see your dataset or your evaluation" | Those live in the workspace they were made in. Add the person to that workspace |
| "That Settings page is missing for me" | The page is not in their role's navigation, and typing the address does not get them in |
| "I clicked it and nothing happened" | Usually a permission rather than a fault. Compare their role against the [permission reference](/docs/roles-and-permissions/reference/permissions) |

<Warning>
Go by your navigation. If a Settings page is not listed there, your role cannot reach it, and the address does not say so. Depending on the page you get a 404, a silent return to Datasets, or the page itself with buttons that do nothing. None of those mean the feature is missing. Ask an Owner or Admin.
</Warning>

The gateway is the one place that breaks this pattern. Its buttons stay live for everyone and refuse on submit with **Write access denied to this workspace**, so somebody who cannot create in a workspace finds out one click later there than anywhere else.

## What the dashboard cannot do

| What you need | Why the dashboard cannot do it |
|---|---|
| A workspace deleted | There is no delete action anywhere in the dashboard |
| A dataset share link withdrawn | Its dialog has no revoke, no expiry and no regenerate. An Observe project can at least be set back to Restricted |
| Someone you removed brought back | Removal marks them Deactivated and the dashboard offers no way to undo it |

Ask the Future AGI team through [support](https://futureagi.com/contact-us) for all three.

A fourth needs settling before you promise anything to a compliance reviewer. The dashboard has no activity or audit log, so you cannot pull a record of who changed whose role. Ask Future AGI whether one exists behind the product before you commit to producing it.

For everything else, an Owner or Admin in your own organization can already do it themselves.

<Note>
**What these pages cover.** They answer who can reach what in the dashboard, and what happens when they try. They do not cover how long a sign-in or token stays valid, what happens to data after an account closes, or what is retained where. Take those to Future AGI rather than reading anything into their absence here.
</Note>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can the same person be in more than one organization?">
    Yes, with a different role in each. The organizations share nothing, and you move between them from the switcher at the top of the sidebar.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can someone hold different roles in different workspaces?">
    Yes. Workspace Admin in one workspace and Workspace Viewer in another is a valid combination, and the difference is visible: the actions on a dataset are live in one and greyed out in the other.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I have an organization role and a workspace role. Which one applies?">
    Owners and Admins are Workspace Admins everywhere. For everyone else the workspace role decides, in both directions. An organization Viewer added as Workspace Member can create, edit and share. An organization Member added as Workspace Viewer cannot.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I clicked Delete Key and nothing happened.">
    Only an Owner can delete a platform API key. Everyone else who can open the page sees the button, gets the confirmation, and is then told **You are not authorized to access this resource**. Disable the key instead, and ask an Owner to delete it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I demoted an Admin to Member. Do they still see every workspace?">
    Only the ones you selected during the demotion. Setting someone to Member or Viewer adds a required Workspaces field, and access to every unselected workspace is removed in the same step.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I am an organization Admin but I was never added to a workspace. Can I see it?">
    Yes. Owners and Admins are Workspace Admins in every workspace. They appear in your workspace switcher without being added.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can a colleague not see the evaluation I built?">
    Evaluations you create belong to the workspace they were created in. Either add your colleague to that workspace, or rebuild the evaluation in one you both belong to. Future AGI's built-in evaluations are always available everywhere.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A Settings page will not open for me. Is it broken?">
    No. If a page is not listed in your Settings navigation, your role cannot reach it.

    Typing the address gets you one of three things and none of them says why: a 404, a silent return to Datasets, or the page itself with buttons that will not work for you. The third gives no sign at all until you click something. Go by your navigation, and ask an Owner or Admin for anything that is not in it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I show something to someone outside my organization?">
    Yes, and which kind of link you get depends on what you are sharing. An **Observe project** can be set to **Restricted**, so only the people you name can open it, and they sign in without joining your organization. A **dataset** has no restricted option: its link is always open to whoever holds the URL, with no account and no sign-in. See [Share outside your organization](/docs/roles-and-permissions/guides/share-outside-your-organization).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do you support SSO?">
    Yes. SAML, along with Google and GitHub sign in, and passkeys. Talk to the Future AGI team before setting SAML up. Once it is configured, password resets are turned off and new sign ups have to come through SAML. Existing password and social logins keep working.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I give one team its own gateway logs?">
    Not inside one organization. Gateway keys and gateway request logs are held at the organization, so gateway traffic is not partitioned by workspace, and every role can open the gateway pages and read those logs.

    If two teams genuinely need separate gateway logs, put them in separate organizations. Organizations share nothing, gateway keys and logs included.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My SDK data is going to the wrong place. What should I check?">
    It is the key. Which half to check depends on what is wrong.

    **Wrong organization:** a key belongs to one organization for good. Selecting a different organization in the dashboard does not change where that key writes, so you need a key made in the other organization.

    **Wrong workspace:** traces land in the workspace the key was created in, and there is no way to redirect them afterwards. Make a new key while you are inside the workspace you want. Other API and SDK calls can name a workspace on the request instead.

    If you never made the key yourself, check whether you are using the starter `system_org_key`. It was never created in a workspace, so its traces go to the organization's default one no matter what you have open.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can a Workspace Admin invite an Admin?">
    No. Their invite carries no Organization Role field, so they grant a workspace role only. Granting an organization role needs an Admin or an Owner.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I bill each workspace separately?">
    Not inside one organization. The plan, the balance, the limits, and the invoice all belong to the organization, and usage is reported per workspace but billed as one. Separate bills mean separate organizations, which also means separate members, workspaces and keys. See [Billing](/docs/billing).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I remove the only Owner?">
    No. Promote a second Owner first, then the original can be demoted or removed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I delete a workspace?">
    You cannot, from the dashboard. There is no delete action on the workspace list or inside a workspace's own settings. Contact the Future AGI team.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens to someone's work after they are removed?">
    It is preserved. Annotations, scores, and other output remain in place, and their sign-in stops immediately.

    Their API keys are the exception and do not stop. A key belongs to the organization rather than the person, so anything still calling with it keeps working until somebody disables it on **Build > Keys**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="An invite expired. What now?">
    Resend it from their row on the Members page. Resending issues a fresh link and restarts the window. Do the same if the invite still shows as Pending but the person says the link is dead.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is there a record of who changed whose role?">
    Not one you can reach. There is no activity or audit log anywhere in the dashboard. Ask the Future AGI team before you commit to producing one for a reviewer.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
